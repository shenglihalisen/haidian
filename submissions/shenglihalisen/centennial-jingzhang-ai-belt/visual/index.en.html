Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

Urban-design submission · offline visual brief (English counterpart of visual/index.html) · login: shenglihalisen

1 · One-line concept

Turn a railway that once carried coal and people into a continuously learning urban line: a 9.67 km slow-mobility spine along the Jing-Zhang railway heritage park becomes a "city learning loop" linking compute, data, scenarios, talent and public life.

3 · Three zones & two wings

Three key areas — Zhongzhiyuan (192.1 ha), Origin Community (104.3 ha), Dazhongsi (72.0 ha) — anchor the "three zones, two wings" structure, totalling 368.4 ha of key design area within the 11.4 km² overall design scope.

4 · East-west reconnection & mobility

7 existing east-west breakpoints are reconnected and 5 new east-west roads are added; the motor-vehicle road-network density reaches 8.68 km/km² (target 8.0, attainment 1.08). The 9.67 km heritage greenway spine provides continuous slow mobility.

5 · AI scenarios & governance

14 AI scenario nodes are placed at concrete points, each with a defined spatial carrier, operator, data boundary and human-review step. Human-review coverage is 100%. All scenario cards are conceptual suggestions; the reference to the Interim Measures for Generative AI Services is a voluntary design-level adoption of its inform-consent-opt-out and human-intervention principles as a precaution — this proposal does not claim to satisfy any compliance obligation under that measure.

6 · Phasing

The phasing layer covers 91.68% of the overall design scope (the remainder is the spine and arterial corridors treated as continuous full-period works). Phasing is a conceptual sequence suggestion, not a development schedule, investment plan or approval sequence.
